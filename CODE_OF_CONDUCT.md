# Code Of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers must act respectfully, assume good faith, give
actionable feedback, and protect private or sensitive information. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not accepted.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers. Do not
publish personal information or confidential material in an issue.

This policy follows the principles of the Contributor Covenant:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
