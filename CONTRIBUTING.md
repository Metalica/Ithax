# Contributing

## Current Policy

Contributions must be original work or include a clear upstream license and
notice. Do not submit proprietary content, extracted client files, credentials,
or generated build output.

Original project-owned contributions are accepted under the MIT License, except
that contributions to the Ithax multicore foundation (Stage 3 and Stage 10
work) and the Ithax TrinityAL Vulkan backend (Stage 5 work) are accepted under
the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 in `LICENSE-POLYFORM`. Carbon changes
must follow the applicable upstream repository rules and licenses.

## Sign-Off

Until a different written agreement is adopted, contributors should use the
Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 and include a `Signed-off-by` trailer on
each commit. Existing history predates this policy and is not rewritten.

## Verification

Run the documented build, CTest suite, and `scripts/quality.ps1` before opening
a change. Keep claims tied to reproducible output from a committed revision.
